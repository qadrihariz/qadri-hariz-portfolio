About Qadri / 01

I’m drawn to design that blends space, image, and story.

I became interested in design in secondary school and studied Interior & Exhibition Design in Nitec, followed by Visual Merchandising in Higher Nitec at the Institute of Technical Education. I’m now studying Visual Communication & Motion Design at Singapore Polytechnic.

My experience includes setting up displays at Massimo Dutti during a Higher Nitec internship, and helping with photoshoots and promotional and educational videos for HomeTeamNS TACTSIM as a part-time team member. In my own time, I create football graphics and explore new creative approaches.
